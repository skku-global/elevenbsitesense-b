How a project works

  1. Site visit or video callWe look at your plot, measure, and listen to what you want.
  2. Written quote and drawingsA fixed price and a timeline in writing, before any work starts.
  3. Build with weekly updatesPhotos and short videos every week, sent to your phone.
  4. HandoverA walk-through, a snag list fixed, and a named person to call afterwards.

Common questions

Can you start without me visiting Nigeria?

Yes. We can survey from your measurements and photos, or meet a family member or representative on site, then agree everything on video.

How do I pay from abroad?

We accept bank transfer and agree the payment schedule in writing. You pay stage by stage as work is completed.

What if I want to change something during the build?

Tell us on WhatsApp. We confirm any change and its cost in writing before we do it.

Start your project from anywhere

Send your plot size, location and ideas. You get a reply within one working day, in your time zone.
