Common questions

How long does it take to build a pool?

A typical home pool takes about 8 to 14 weeks, depending on size, soil and finish. You get a written timeline with your quote.

How much does a swimming pool cost?

It depends on size, depth, finish and equipment. We give a fixed written quote after a site visit or a call with your measurements, so you know the price before work starts.

Can I manage the build from abroad?

Yes. We send weekly photo and video updates and tie payments to finished stages. Our Build from abroad page explains the full process.
